# Example papers

The app includes six representative curated reactions from the following papers and their Supporting Information:

| Paper | DOI | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nicewicz & MacMillan, Science 322, 77–80 (2008) | https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1161976 | Aldehyde α-alkylation, Table 1 entries 3 and 4 |
| Zuo et al., Science 345, 437–440 (2014) | https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1255525 | Decarboxylative arylation, Fig. 3 compounds 10 and 14 |
| Horn et al., Nature 533, 77–81 (2016) | https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17431 | Electrochemical allylic oxidation, compounds 6 and 7 |

Papers open at their DOI links. The desktop package contains citations, molecular structures, reaction conditions and evidence locators; no full papers. Two Nicewicz records remain drafts because article/SI yields disagree. The examples are partial assisted curation, not a complete substrate scope or independent experimental reproduction.
